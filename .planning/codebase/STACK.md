## Configuration

**Build:**

## Platform Requirements

**Development:**
- Python 3.11+ and pip; `uv` recommended (used by CI and for the lockfile).
- NVIDIA GPU with a working `nvidia-smi` for accelerated local inference (`core/resource_telemetry.py:62-90`); degrades to CPU/`None` cleanly.
- ~4 GB VRAM ceiling: one LLM/VLM active at a time, enforced by `InferenceGate` (`core/local_llm.py:36-100`); llama-server launched with `-c 4096 -ngl 28`.
- Writable `models/` (~2 GB GGUF pair), `data/`, `.cache/`, `tools/llama-server/`.
- Internet access required **only** for first-run asset acquisition (`python setup.py`) — not for normal operation.

**Production:**
- Deployment target is a single-user laptop (`start.py:58-90` refuses non-loopback Streamlit binds unless `--allow-non-loopback` or `RAG_ALLOW_NON_LOOPBACK=1`).
- Docker path present but still loopback-published (`docker-compose.yml:8`) and explicitly documented as unauthenticated.
- No serverless/PaaS target detected; no Kubernetes, Terraform, or cloud IaC in the tree.
